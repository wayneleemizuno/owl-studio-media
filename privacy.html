Privacy policy

OWL Studio uploader · Last updated 8 November 2026

What the uploader is

The OWL Studio uploader is a private tool used only by the person who runs OWL Studio. It uploads OWL Studio's own videos, subtitles and thumbnails to OWL Studio's own YouTube channel, and posts replies to comments there once that person has approved each one. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.

YouTube API Services

The uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses and stores

  • It signs in to the OWL Studio YouTube account through Google, with only the permissions shown on Google's sign-in screen: uploading videos, and managing subtitles and comments on that channel.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never shared, sold or sent anywhere except to Google's APIs.
  • It collects no data about viewers or any other user, and uses no cookies or tracking.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party connections page, or from Google's security settings. Removing it stops the uploader working, and the stored token is then deleted from the owner's computer.
